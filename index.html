Supplier Management

Automatically track and verify purchase orders with vendors. Receive notifications for delays or cancellations, including weather-related setbacks using our integrated Weather API.

  • Milestone auto-check-in
  • Supplier performance analysis based on on-time deliveries

Marketing Alignment

AI estimates shipment arrivals and calculates key marketing dates. Stay ahead with automated alerts for shipment delays, a shared campaign calendar, and a real-time visibility board for order status.

  • Shared calendar for campaign alignment
  • Real-time order visibility
  • Automated shipment delay alerts

Inventory Management

Leverage AI for demand planning during major events and monitor customer behavior patterns to forecast inventory needs. Automated reordering helps prevent stockouts, with priority given to top-selling products.

  • Event-based demand forecasting
  • Automated reordering at set thresholds
  • Continuous auditing of top-selling items

Customer Service Automation

Enhance customer experience with an AI-powered chatbot that manages inquiries, order changes, and cancellations. The system even recommends alternative items when products are out of stock and generates automated complaint reports.

  • AI-driven chatbot support
  • Automated complaint analysis & resolution
  • Order change and cancellation handling
